I study how AI can learn meaningful, robust representations from complex multimodal and structured data.

I am a PhD student at Harbin Institute of Technology, supervised by Prof. Xiaopeng Fan. My research spans multimodal representation learning, trustworthy foundation models, and AI for science, with a particular interest in single-cell and biological data.
